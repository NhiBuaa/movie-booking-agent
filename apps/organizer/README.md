# Cinema Organizer Portal (`apps/organizer`)

Web management portal for cinema operators, theater managers, and movie distributors.

## Responsibilities
- **Movie Catalog & Assets**: Upload movie details, synopsis, age ratings, high-res posters, and video trailers.
- **Auditoriums & Seating Layouts**: Create and configure theater halls, seat matrices (Standard, VIP, Sweetbox couples, accessible seats).
- **Showtime Scheduling**: Multi-hall calendar/timeline view, assign movies to time slots, collision detection.
- **Ticket Pricing & Promotions**: Define standard and surge pricing, bundle combos (popcorn, drinks), manage voucher campaigns.
- **Box Office Analytics**: Real-time sales, auditorium occupancy rates, daily/weekly revenue reports, export for reconciliation.
